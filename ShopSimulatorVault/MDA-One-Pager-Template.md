## One-line pitch
This game is for people who love management and simulator games. The player talks to customers, takes in donations as stock, cleans and prices the donations, and organises shelves. The player gets a sense of satisfaction seeing their shop grow.

## Aesthetics (what the player feels)
- management
- time-pressure
- progression

## Core mechanics (3 to 5 verbs or systems)
1. Balance time with profit and rating
2. Clean/Fix Donations
3. Price/Sell Donations
4. Organise Stock
5. Talk with Customers

## Dynamics (what emerges when the mechanics meet the player)
- Clean/Fixed donations are worth more than dirty ones
- Customers who are seen promptly and are treated well by the player will spend more money
- If a customer waits too long, the shop rating will go down
- If a donation is priced too high, customers wont buy them and the rating will go down. If its priced too low, the shop will lose money.
